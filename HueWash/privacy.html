Hue Wash Privacy Policy

Privacy Policy

Last updated: July 5, 2026

Hue Wash is designed as a simple offline puzzle game. The app does not require an account and does not collect personal information from players.

Information Hue Wash stores

Hue Wash may store gameplay information locally on your device, such as your progress, saved game state, and whether the tutorial has been seen. This information stays on your device and is not sent to the developer.

Analytics, ads, and tracking

Hue Wash does not use third-party analytics, advertising SDKs, or tracking features. The app does not access your contacts, photos, camera, microphone, or precise location.

Support requests

If you contact support through GitHub or email, any information you choose to provide will be used only to respond to the request. Please avoid sharing sensitive personal information in public support requests.

Changes to this policy

This policy may be updated when Hue Wash changes. The latest version will be posted on this page.

Contact

For privacy questions, contact poyraz@bariskan.com.
